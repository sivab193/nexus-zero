One route. Both directions.

Nexus Zero uses the first word as a semantic registry switch. Every word after it is a base-4096 digit carrying the destination payload. Because the arithmetic is reversible, the browser needs no saved URL mapping.

URL → canonical form → registry slot + BigInt → words
words → registry slot + BigInt → canonical form → URL

Why the first word matters.

WORD 01

Registry switch

video means YouTube. global means a .com domain. nexus is the root of siv19.dev.

WORDS 02…N

Payload digits

Characters are packed with the smallest compatible alphabet and expressed as 12-bit dictionary indices.

UPPER HALF

Path signal

The paired word 2,048 positions later signals the same realm with a path or extended payload.

A Library of Babel for the web.

Borges imagined a library containing every possible book. Nexus Zero’s coordinate field similarly contains every representable route—whether anyone has dialed it before or not. The Babel Dice lets you explore that space, while reserved registry slots stop safely until they are assigned.
